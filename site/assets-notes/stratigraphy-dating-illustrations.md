# Stratigraphy, dating, and past environments illustrations

Eight original, editable SVGs accompany the English primary article and its
Chinese translation in `personal/stratigraphy-dating-and-past-environments`.
Each of the four diagrams appears at the same position in both versions,
with equivalent language-specific labels and captions.

Assets: `site/public/images/personal/stratigraphy/{sequence,sample-event,environment,causation}-{en,zh}.svg`.

The illustrations are authored vector diagrams, not photographs, archaeological
reconstructions, excavation drawings, measured plots, or third-party images.
They contain no measurements or sample data. The hypothetical settlement's
events A, B, C, D correspond to 甲, 乙, 丙, 丁 in Chinese. The pit cut is a
negative interface; its fill is later than the older floor that the pit cuts.
Arrows express sequence, not duration. The environmental diagrams identify
types of archives and checks, without claiming that every proxy has a fixed
spatial range or temporal resolution.

All SVGs have a 1200 × 760 viewBox, an accessible title and description, and
a fixed light background so their labels remain readable in either site theme.
HTML dimensions reserve space; a keyboard-focusable horizontal region retains
legible diagram labels on narrow screens. Assets are loaded lazily.

Method references appear beside the relevant discussion in the article:
Harris's account of archaeological stratigraphy, Historic England's radiocarbon
and environmental archaeology guidance, Oxford's calibration explanation,
NOAA's proxy data introduction, and the cited original chronology and Liangzhu
papers. No source figure or copyrighted illustration was copied or traced.
