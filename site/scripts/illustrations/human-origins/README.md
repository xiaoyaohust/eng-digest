# Human origins article illustrations

Original conceptual diagrams for the bilingual essay in the Chinese history
column. No specimen photographs, fossil reconstructions, facial reconstructions,
measured sequence traces, estimated ancestry proportions, or migration maps are
reproduced. Colored molecules and the generic pedigree are teaching symbols.

Run `python site/scripts/illustrations/human-origins/generate.py` in an isolated
environment with matplotlib installed. The script uses the existing bundled
Noto Sans SC font and converts text to paths. The twelve committed SVGs are
article source assets served directly; the website build does not run Python
or require matplotlib.

## Factual references for the diagrams

* Method and information scope:
  Orlando et al., *Ancient DNA analysis* (2021),
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-020-00011-0
* DNA preservation and authentication context:
  Dabney, Meyer, and Pääbo, *Ancient DNA Damage* (2013),
  https://doi.org/10.1101/cshperspect.a012567
* Direct parentage of Denisova 11:
  Slon et al. (2018), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0455-x
* The Xiahe mandible and its minimum age:
  Chen et al. (2019), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1139-x
* Harbin proteins and minimum age:
  Fu et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adu9677
* Harbin mitochondrial DNA from dental calculus:
  Fu et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.05.040

References were checked on November 22, 2026. The evidence comparison describes
the Xiahe mandible, not all remains or sediment samples from Baishiya. Reported
age minima are not exact ages. The family diagram states a result for one
individual, without estimating exchange rates between entire populations.
